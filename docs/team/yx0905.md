### Project: Astra

Astra is a command-line contact book for people who network a lot. Users capture each new person they meet (name, company, role, phone, email and LinkedIn) in the seconds between conversations, then pull them up again before they next meet. The user interacts with it using a CLI, and it has a GUI created with JavaFX. It is written in Java.

Given below are my contributions to the project.

* **New Feature**: Interaction logging with `log`, `unlog` and `editlog` (Pull requests [\#172](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/172), [\#173](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/173), [\#182](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/182), [\#207](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/207))
  * What it does: records a dated note of each conversation or meeting with a contact, keeps the history in date order, lets the user remove an interaction logged by mistake (after confirmation), and correct its date or note.
  * Justification: Astra's users meet many people at events. Remembering what was discussed is what makes the next conversation useful, so the history of each contact is central to the product.
  * Highlights: contacts can be chosen by index or by part of their name, with a numbered choice when several match. Entries are re-sorted and renumbered after each change, future dates and duplicate notes are rejected, and all of it is saved and restored with the contact file.

* **New Feature**: Searching interaction notes with `find /notes` (Pull request [\#174](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/174))
  * What it does: finds contacts whose logged notes mention any of the given keywords, and shows the date and an excerpt of each matching note.
  * Justification: users often remember what was talked about rather than who said it.

* **New Feature**: Command shortcuts with `alias` (Pull requests [\#175](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/175), [\#176](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/176))
  * What it does: lets users save a short word for a command they type often, such as `fe` for `find /email`, then list, replace or remove shortcuts. Every result starts with `Ran:` and the full command, so users can see what ran.
  * Highlights: shortcuts are validated so they cannot shadow built-in commands or chain to other shortcuts, are saved in the preferences file immediately, and run through the same checks and confirmations as the full command.

* **New Feature**: Completing and reopening follow-ups with `completefollowup` and `reopenfollowup` (Pull request [\#206](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103-F13-2/tp/pull/206))
